Meet the Creator

I'm Roberto! Born and raised in West Michigan, I am a bilingual web developer with a passion for creating an immersive digital experience. Here is where I am able to display my whimsical nonsense in the most structured and professional way.

Why Saturno?

This project was inspired by: Saturn. The classical ruling planet of the signs Capricorn and Aquarius. I quite literally looked to the stars to find a name. My birth chart features a Capricorn Sun and an Aquarius Stellium.

I felt that Capricorn represents the part that wants things built properly and patiently, much like this website. Aquarius represents the part of me that is more unconventional and contrarian leading me to develop many projects that no one has asked for.
