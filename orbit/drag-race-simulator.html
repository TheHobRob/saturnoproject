Drag Race Simulator

A data-driven simulation modeling contestant scoring and elimination logic.

Web DevEntertainment

Live Demo — coming soon Read the Devlog →

Built to explore how structured data and a handful of scoring rules can recreate the drama of an elimination-based reality competition — episode-by-episode placements, running point totals, and a bottom-of-the-pack cutoff that decides who's safe.

The devlog series covers the build itself: how the scoring schema came together, what the track-record data looks like under the hood, and the decisions behind turning a season's worth of placements into something simulatable.
