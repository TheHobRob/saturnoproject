Preguntas

FAQ

Planning a big night?

For parties of 9 or more, or to book the patio for a private event, give us a call and we'll set it up.

Reservations & large parties

Do you take reservations?

Yes. Book online for parties of up to 8. Walk-ins are always welcome, and we hold about half the dining room and all of the bar for them.

How do I book for a large group?

For 9 to 25 guests, call us at least three days ahead. We'll hold a section and can set up a family-style taco spread.

Can I rent the patio for a private event?

The patio holds up to 60 guests standing or 40 seated. Birthdays, rehearsal dinners, and work parties are all fair game. Call or email for packages.

Dietary options

Do you have vegetarian or vegan dishes?

Plenty. Look for the V on the menu. Our coliflor al carbón tacos and enchiladas verdes can be made vegan on request.

Is the menu gluten-free friendly?

Our corn tortillas are made in-house and are gluten-free. The fryer is shared, so chips and churros are not safe for celiac guests.

What about food allergies?

Tell your server and the kitchen will walk you through it. Our mole poblano contains peanuts, almonds, and sesame.

Policies

Do I need an ID?

Yes, to order alcohol. After 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays the whole place is 21 and up.

Is there a dress code?

No. Come as you are. Flip-flops on the patio are standard.

Can I bring my dog?

Leashed, friendly dogs are welcome on the patio. We'll bring out a water bowl.

Is there a cover charge?

Never on regular nights. A few special events, like the mezcal tasting, are ticketed and marked on the events calendar.

Road work on Calle Verde

Calle Verde is closed between 5th and 6th St for utility work through mid-November. Coming from the west, take Main Ave to 6th St, then turn left onto Calle Verde. The lot entrance on Main Ave stays open.

Parking

  • Free lot behind the building, 32 spaces. Enter from Main Ave.
  • Street parking on Calle Verde is free after 6pm.
  • Bike racks next to the patio gate.

Getting here

  • Bus 22 stops at Calle Verde & 7th St, a one-minute walk.
  • Rideshare drop-off is at the front door.
  • Two blocks from Plaza Verde.
